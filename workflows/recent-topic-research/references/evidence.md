# Evidence and synthesis

Retain a compact evidence ledger: URL, source, author when available, publication time, event date if different, one relevant claim and retrieval mode (native API or web index). For large responses, use available local artifact/file facilities and keep only the ledger in working context. Do not assume the MCP tool supports a storage option it does not expose.

Deduplicate normalized URLs and cross-posted claims. Several pages repeating one press release are one underlying source, not independent corroboration. Cluster evidence by development or reaction, keeping contradictory observations visible. Prioritize relevance, date fit, primary evidence and independent corroboration; engagement alone is not reliability. Do not manufacture confidence percentages or treat sample counts as platform-wide trends.

Exclude clearly out-of-window items from recent findings. Undated material can supply labelled background but cannot establish that something happened in the requested period. An old event reported recently should not be described as a new event.

Return a concise brief with:
- Explicit date window and sources actually queried.
- Three to five themes or developments, each with representative source links and clear fact versus interpretation.
- Important disagreement or minority feedback where present.
- Coverage gaps, substitutions and uncertainty about dates or representativeness.

Scale the number of findings down when evidence is thin. Absence of retrieved discussion does not prove absence of discussion. Stop once the requested question is answered; do not add recurring monitoring unless separately requested.
